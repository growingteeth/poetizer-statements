Poetizer Statements Est. October 24, 2026

The poems are still in there. Nobody can open the door.

Poetizer — a poetry app that said it held 3,000,000+ poems from 330,000+ users — has been unreachable for about a month. There has been no announcement, no timeline, no way to export a single line. Writers, many of them paying subscribers, are locked out of years of their own work. This page collects their statements, in their own words, so the scale of the loss is on the record.

Grey

Founding statement — started this page

I've had Poetizer since 2024, and in that time I published dozens and dozens of poems on it. I honestly can't tell you the exact number, and that's part of the loss — there's no way to open the app and count what I wrote. Only a portion of the app's interface shows, and if you try to click on any poem published by yourself or others, it will only show a blank page. Even trying to scroll through your published poem catalog, it will only show a couple until it stops letting you scroll.

I'm a paying subscriber. It's $1.99 a month, and it renewed again on October 12 — weeks after the app had already stopped working. The money still goes out. The poems are still in there, or at least, they should be. I just can't get to them, and nobody at Poetizer has said a single word about why, or whether they're ever coming back.

What happened

A month of silence

  1. ~Late Sept 2026

    Poetizer stops loading

    The app and site become unreachable. The most common report on the unofficial status tracker is “Nothing is loading” — users say they can't log in and can't pull up their completed work. Reports come in from the US, India, Germany, the UK, Canada, and beyond.

  2. Oct 12, 2026

    Subscriptions keep renewing

    Grey's $1.99 monthly subscription renews as usual — for a service that no longer loads. Nothing in the outage has stopped the billing.

  3. Oct 17, 2026

    Writers organize

    A Change.org petition, “Demand accountability and refunds from Poetizer,” is published. Its author describes losing 200 poems spanning 4–5 years, and says messages to the developers have gone unanswered.

  4. Oct 24, 2026

    Still down. Still silent.

    The unofficial status tracker shows Poetizer down for about a month. As of this page's publication, no official shutdown or restoration statement from Poetizer has been found — no announcement, no timeline, no export tool.

What writers are asking for

Three reasonable things

Nobody is asking for the impossible. The writers who trusted Poetizer with their work are asking for this:

  1. Give us our work back

    Restore access to the platform — or, if that's genuinely impossible, provide a way for every writer to export their own poems. The work belongs to the people who wrote it.

  2. Say something

    An actual statement: what happened, whether the poems still exist, and what happens next. A month of silence toward hundreds of thousands of users is itself a decision, and it's the cruelest part of this.

  3. Refund the subscriptions

    Subscribers paid for a service that stopped working and kept being charged anyway. Refund the subscription and purchase money taken while the platform was unreachable.
